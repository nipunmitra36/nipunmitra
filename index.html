01 · About

A bit about me

I started out building websites with plain HTML, CSS and Bootstrap at Mamurjor IT, shipping more than twenty client sites. Today I work at CodeWare Ltd, where I build Next.js apps, SaaS dashboards and university and corporate websites.

I care about the small stuff — spacing, states, how a page feels on a cheap phone. I like breaking big UIs into reusable components, wiring them up to real APIs, and lately I've been exploring AI-integrated interfaces with tools like Ollama.

I speak Bengali natively and work in English professionally. When I'm not coding, I'm finishing my B.Sc. in CSE at Northern University.
